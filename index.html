Josh Patel's Noodle Recipe

Ingredients

  1. 1 pack of Maggi Noodles
  2. get a pot and full it half way or more if for more people, with low heat for 3 minutes
  3. Add 1 3/4 tsp of salt and 1 1/2 tbsp of red chili powder
  4. Add 1/2 tbsp of Garam Masala(aka warm spice mix)
  5. Add your noodles and with its seasoning pack for more flavor
  6. Let it cook for 5-7 minutes until it turns yellow
  7. Turn off the stove and add 1 3/4 tbsp of schezwan sauce
  8. Add a knife full of butter and mix until it melts
  9. Wait 45 seconds for it to cool down
  10. (extra: you can add any topping you want)
